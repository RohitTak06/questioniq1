How QuestionIQ Works

From raw PDF question papers to transparent, explainable exam analytics.

01

OCR & Question Segmentation

Scanned question papers are processed using Tesseract OCR, cleaning noise and isolating individual questions, marks, and sub-parts.

91.3% Extraction Accuracy
02

NLP Topic Modeling (NMF)

Non-Negative Matrix Factorization identifies recurring curriculum themes and maps every question to its core academic domain.

12 Discovered Topic Clusters
03

Explainable Difficulty (SHAP)

Random Forest classifier predicts question complexity (Easy, Medium, Hard) and computes exact SHAP feature contributions explaining why.
