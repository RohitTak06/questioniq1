Model Caveats & Academic Transparency

Difficulty is predicted using a Random Forest model trained on dual-annotated CS and AS previous-year questions. Predictions are based on structural features (Bloom's taxonomy action verbs, technical vocabulary density, marks weight, mathematical expressions, sub-parts, and topic affiliation). SHAP feature contributions explain why the model made a given choice; they represent model attribution signals, not definitive proof of academic difficulty.
